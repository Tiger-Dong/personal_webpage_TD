  1. Engineering工程开发2026.05.26 – 08.08

    Accessible Michelson Interferometer无障碍迈克耳孙干涉仪控制系统

    System developer · Beijing Jiaotong University, Beijing系统开发 · 北京交通大学，北京

    I built a voice- and text-controlled system that combines speech recognition and language-model-assisted command processing with Arduino boards and six stepper motors. It operates coarse and fine adjustment and the two-axis alignment of both mirrors, checking the target, direction, speed, and movement amount before execution.我将语音识别、大语言模型辅助指令处理与 Arduino、六组步进电机连接，开发语音和文字控制系统，实现粗调、细调与两面反射镜的双轴调节。系统在执行前核对目标、方向、速度与运动量，并拒绝不明确的指令。

    The system reduces direct contact with a sensitive optical instrument and is designed to make experiments easier for students with limited hand mobility.系统减少了对精密光学仪器的直接接触，旨在降低对手部精细动作的依赖，让手部活动受限的学生更容易参与实验。

    Independent astronomy study · Beijing天文学自主学习 · 北京

    I used AI-assisted programming to study two astronomy papers and work with public Hubble and Chandra observations. My exploration included image processing, gravitational-lensing signals, and Kaiser–Squires mass reconstruction.我借助 AI 辅助编程阅读两篇天文学论文，处理哈勃与钱德拉公开观测资料，探索图像处理、引力透镜信号和 Kaiser–Squires 质量重建方法。

    Polyurethane Simulation Frontend聚氨酯材料模拟平台前端

    Frontend developer · Beijing Jiaotong University前端开发 · 北京交通大学

    I built the interface connecting a material-simulation backend with factory users: material input, task creation, progress monitoring, searchable records, and result charts. I introduced table-based formulation input, three-decimal precision, constrained fields, and time-ordered task records.我为材料模拟后台开发面向工厂用户的界面，涵盖材料输入、任务创建、进度监控、记录搜索与结果图表，并改进表格式配方输入、三位小数精度、字段限制和按时间排序的任务记录。

    The platform supports formulation studies for railway elastic pads, allowing users to compare simulation results before committing materials to physical trials. The team's platform is registered as Polyurethane Material Performance Simulation Platform V1.0, registration 2025SR0074630.平台服务于铁路弹性垫板的配方研究，让用户先比较模拟结果，再安排实际材料试验，减少试错成本。团队平台的软著名称为「聚氨酯弹性材料性能计算模拟平台 V1.0」，登记号 2025SR0074630。
